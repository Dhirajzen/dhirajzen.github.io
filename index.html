Interview prep

FIFO Depth Calculation: 8 Interview Traps (and How to Beat Them)

One idea answers every version of “how deep must this FIFO be?”: it only has to hold the backlog that builds while writes outpace reads. It solves the standard case, then works through eight traps, from idle cycles and the 2X trap to back-to-back bursts and async margin.

Clock domain crossing

Async FIFO Explained: Gray Code, Synchronizers and the Full-Flag Twist

Builds an asynchronous FIFO step by step to show how data crosses safely between two unrelated clocks: the lap bit, metastability and the two-flop synchronizer, why a binary pointer going from 7 to 8 can arrive as 15, and how Gray code fixes it.

Reliability & ECC

The Error Nobody Notices: How Chips Survive a Bit Flip

A 2003 Belgian election gave one candidate exactly 4,096 extra votes: 212, a single flipped bit. What a bit flip is, how chips survive one with parity and ECC, and how verification engineers use fault injection to prove every flip gets fixed or caught.
